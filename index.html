Leonardo Iturriaga Tisoc

Mechatronics Engineering student · PUCP · Lima, Peru

I am a Mechatronics Engineering student at Pontificia Universidad Católica del Perú (PUCP). I like building robots end to end: designing the mechanics in CAD, printing and assembling the hardware, writing the embedded and ROS 2 software, and training the controllers in simulation.

Right now most of my time goes into legged robots: a 12-DOF biped I designed from scratch and a five-legged robot, both trained to walk with reinforcement learning in MuJoCo. I also build a mobile manipulator with a voice-controlled AI agent, and a multi-robot simulation for a research project at PUCP.

Bipedo: 12-DOF Bipedal Robot, from CAD to Reinforcement Learning

A 3D-printed, 46 cm biped with twelve Feetech STS3215 servos and a Raspberry Pi 5. I designed it in Inventor, generate its MuJoCo model straight from the CAD, and trained a walking policy with mjlab that also walks in Gazebo. The right leg is built and tested on hardware.
