Projects

What I am building right now. Most of these are still in progress, so each page says what works today and what comes next. Questions, ideas and feedback are welcome.

In progress

Bipedo: 12-DOF Bipedal Robot, from CAD to Reinforcement Learning

A 3D-printed, 46 cm biped with twelve Feetech STS3215 servos and a Raspberry Pi 5. I designed it in Inventor, generate its MuJoCo model straight from the CAD, and trained a walking policy with mjlab that also walks in Gazebo. The right leg is built and tested on hardware.
In progress

Pentabot: Five-Legged Robot Trained to Walk Without Encoders

A 15-DOF five-legged robot with hobby MG995 servos, which give no position feedback. I modeled it in MuJoCo from the CAD and trained a PPO policy that walks using only the IMU, the joystick command and its own past actions, so it can run on the real hardware.
In progress

ArmCar: Mobile Manipulator with a Voice-Controlled AI Agent

A wheeled robot with an SO-101 arm, split between a PC (brain) and a Raspberry Pi 5 (body) over ROS 2 Jazzy. It is driven with a DualSense controller, joint by joint or with inverse kinematics, and has a Claude-based agent you talk to by voice that can look through a webcam and moves the real arm.
Research

Swarmbots: Summit XL Swarm that Sorts Trash by Color

A ROS 2 Jazzy + Gazebo Harmonic simulation of mecanum Summit XLS robots with grippers and RGB-D cameras that pick up trash and deliver it to the depot of its color. The classic pipeline works; the goal is to record demonstrations and train an ACT imitation-learning policy.
